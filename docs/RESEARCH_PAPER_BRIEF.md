**Purpose:** This document gives an AI agent full context to draft a conference paper: objectives, methodology, data schema, figure list with paths and captions, table references, and outcomes. Cite all figures by path and caption when writing the paper.

## 1. Project and suggested title

- **Project name:** EduSync  
- **Suggested paper title:** *Edge AI–Powered Learning Analytics: Vision-Based Attention and Multimodal Engagement*  
- **Scope:** BTech ECE final year capstone (12 credits); evaluation over ~1 month; data from real usage plus synthetic multi-user data for scalability.

---

## 2. Two research objectives (copy/paraphrase into abstract and introduction)

**Objective 1 — Edge AI performance and vision-based attention**  
Demonstrate that an edge-deployed LMS (Llama-3 on GPU, vision/OCR on CPU) achieves: (a) measurable inference performance—latency and throughput (chars/s) for OCR, summary, flashcards, and quiz generation, with metrics suitable for tables; (b) vision-based attention monitoring—head-pose estimation (MediaPipe + solvePnP, Moving Average smoothing) producing stable yaw/pitch/roll and a binary attention state (focused vs distracted) logged per frame, with summary statistics and time-series/distribution plots.

**Objective 2 — Multimodal engagement and correlation with quiz performance**  
Demonstrate that: (a) scroll-based engagement (DSP pipeline: FIR, energy, ZCR, reading/idle/skimming ratios) yields a Scroll Engagement Score (0–100) logged per session; (b) vision-based attention (focused ratio per quiz session) and scroll engagement correlate with quiz score (learning outcome) across multiple users/sessions, with scatter plots and Pearson r (and p-values), with sufficient N (e.g. ≥20–30 pairs) for a short conference paper.

## 3. Methodology (short overview for paper)

- **Architecture:** Edge node (local server/laptop); no cloud. Split-compute: LLM (Llama-3 8B Q4) on GPU; vision (MediaPipe Face Mesh + OpenCV solvePnP) and OCR (EasyOCR) on CPU so GPU remains free for the LLM.
- **Vision pipeline:** Camera frames at 2 Hz during quiz → MediaPipe 2D landmarks → 3D face model + solvePnP → yaw, pitch, roll (degrees). Moving Average over last 10 frames per student; threshold rule: |yaw| > 20° or |pitch| > 15° → distracted (0), else focused (1). Logged to `attention_log.csv` with `student_id`, `assignment_id`, `timestamp_iso`.
- **DSP pipeline (scroll):** Scroll velocity at 1 Hz during material viewing → FIR filter, energy, ZCR, FFT → reading/idle/skimming ratios → Scroll Engagement Score (0–100). Logged to `engagement_metrics.jsonl` with `user_id`, `material_id`, `engagement_score`, `session_duration_s`, and DSP fields.
- **Data collection:** Real app usage (inference, attention, scroll). For scalable evaluation, synthetic multi-user data generated by `backend/scripts/generate_synthetic_research_data.py` (reproducible **seed 42**): seeds DB with synthetic users, classrooms, materials, assignments, quiz submissions; overwrites `attention_log.csv` and `engagement_metrics.jsonl` with data designed so engagement/attention correlate with quiz score. Then run `export_research_csv.py`, `analyze_attention.py`, `visualize_research_data.py` to produce CSVs and figures.

## 4. Data and schema (where to find numbers and tables)

All research artifacts live under **`backend/research_data/`**.

| Artifact | Path | Purpose |
|----------|------|--------|
| Attention log (raw) | `backend/research_data/attention_log.csv` | Per-frame head pose and attention state |
| Attention summary | `backend/research_data/attention_summary.csv` | Per (student, assignment): mean yaw/pitch/roll, focused_ratio |
| Attention vs quiz | `backend/research_data/attention_vs_quiz.csv` | Paired focused_ratio and quiz_score for correlation |
| Engagement (raw) | `backend/research_data/engagement_metrics.jsonl` | Per-session scroll engagement and DSP metrics |
| Engagement (CSV) | `backend/research_data/engagement_metrics.csv` | Same, exported to CSV |
| Paired engagement–quiz | `backend/research_data/paired_engagement_quiz.csv` | Per (user, material): avg_engagement_score, quiz_score |
| Inference metrics | `backend/research_data/inference_metrics.jsonl` / `.csv` | Per-call: operation, duration_ms, input/output chars |
| Latency percentiles | `backend/research_data/latency_percentiles.csv` | p50/p95 etc. per operation |
| Metrics summary | `backend/research_data/metrics_summary.csv` | Aggregated inference/engagement counts |

**attention_log.csv columns:** `timestamp`, `timestamp_iso`, `student_id`, `yaw`, `pitch`, `roll`, `attention_state`, `assignment_id`  
**engagement_metrics (JSONL) key fields:** `timestamp`, `user_id`, `material_id`, `engagement_score`, `session_duration_s`, `scroll_engagement_score`, `reading_ratio`, `idle_ratio`, `skimming_ratio`, plus DSP fields (energy, zcr, etc.)  
**attention_vs_quiz.csv columns:** `student_id`, `assignment_id`, `full_name`, `focused_ratio`, `quiz_score`, `sample_count`  
**paired_engagement_quiz.csv columns:** `user_id`, `material_id`, `full_name`, `avg_engagement_score`, `quiz_score`, `engagement_sessions`, `quiz_submissions`

## 6. Tables (source CSVs and how to fill)

- **Table 1 — Inference performance (Edge AI):** Fill from `backend/research_data/inference_metrics.csv` (or aggregate from `inference_metrics.jsonl`). Columns: Operation, Count, Avg duration (ms), Avg input (chars), Avg output (chars), Chars/s. Run `backend/scripts/export_research_csv.py` to (re)generate. For percentiles use `latency_percentiles.csv`.
- **Table 2 — Attention metrics (Vision):** Fill from `backend/research_data/attention_log.csv`; overall stats are printed by `backend/scripts/analyze_attention.py` and per-session stats are in `attention_summary.csv`. Columns: Metric (Yaw deg, Pitch deg, Roll deg, Focused ratio), Mean, Std, Min, Max.

## 7. Outcomes to stress in the paper

1. **Objective 1:** Edge inference and vision metrics with concrete numbers—latency and throughput per operation (Table 1), head-pose statistics and focused ratio (Table 2), and figures (latency bar chart, attention time series, attention distribution).
2. **Objective 2:** Positive correlation between engagement/attention and quiz score—report N (e.g. 60 paired engagement–quiz, 120 attention–quiz pairs), Pearson r and p-values from scatter plots (Figures 1 and 6), and note that synthetic data was used for scalability with seed 42 for reproducibility.
